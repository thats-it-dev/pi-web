Second color, from Wada's Dictionary of Color Combinations

Each option is a color Sanzo Wada paired with an orange very close to kit's #ffaa00. Light mode uses his swatch as printed; dark mode uses a lighter version of the same hue. All four are clearly distinct from orange on the colorblind check in both themes. The strip under each name is the original combination.

1. Violet

#4f4086 · his most frequent orange partner (combos 42, 156, 164, 335). Deep and inky; the quietest of the four in light mode.

2. Blue

#006eb8 · combos 191, 257, 267. A clearer, mid blue — brighter than kit's navy --secondary.

3. Peacock Blue

#00939b · combo 286, with Burnt Sienna and Violet Blue. A teal with more ink than last round's.

4. Rosolanc Purple

#b73f74 · combo 170, with Red Violet. A warm magenta; the boldest pairing and the least 'default chart'.
